About me

Building cloud platforms with reliability, security, and operational clarity in mind.

I am a DevOps and Platform Engineer focused on building secure, repeatable, and observable systems on AWS. My work spans cloud architecture, infrastructure as code, continuous delivery, container platforms, networking, security, and operational automation.

Across my projects, I have designed AWS foundations with Terraform, created Jenkins delivery pipelines, implemented GitOps workflows with Argo CD, operated Kubernetes and ECS platforms, and built monitoring and autoscaling capabilities using Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, and Kubernetes HPA.

I approach platform engineering as more than deploying infrastructure. I consider how a system will be secured, observed, recovered, maintained, and operated within realistic cost constraints.

  • Automation over manual repetition
  • Security and recovery by design
  • Observable production systems
  • Cost-aware architecture decisions

Engineering approach

02

03

Establish clear boundaries

04

Design for recovery

Treat rollback, infrastructure reconstruction, backups, and disaster recovery as architecture.
